# User Guide
## How to Use the Crop Disease Detection System

### Step 1 - Open the Dashboard
- Open your web browser
- Go to the dashboard link provided by your technician
- You will see the main page with an upload button

### Step 2 - Upload a Leaf Photo
- Click the Upload button
- Select a clear photo of the crop leaf from your phone or computer
- Make sure the leaf fills most of the photo
- Good lighting gives better results

### Step 3 - Check for Disease
- Click the Check for Disease button
- Wait 2 to 3 seconds for the AI to analyze
- The result will appear on the screen

### Step 4 - Read the Result
- Disease name will be shown at the top
- Confidence percentage shows how sure the AI is
- Severity level tells you how urgent the problem is
  - HIGH means act immediately
  - MEDIUM means monitor closely
  - LOW means keep watching

### Step 5 - Follow Treatment Advice
- Scroll down to see treatment steps
- Follow each step carefully
- Contact your local agriculture office if problem gets worse

## Tips for Best Results
- Take photos in good natural light
- Make sure the leaf is clean and clearly visible
- Take close up photos of the affected area
- Use one leaf per photo for accurate results
